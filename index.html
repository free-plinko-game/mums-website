About Me

Hello! I'm Karen, a fully qualified, DVSA approved driving instructor based in Stalybridge, passionate about helping learners become safe, confident drivers.

I believe that everyone learns differently, which is why I tailor my teaching approach to suit your individual needs. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to brush up on your skills, I'll help you progress at a pace that's comfortable for you.

My lessons are conducted in a calm, patient and encouraging environment. I understand that learning to drive can be nerve-wracking, so I focus on building your confidence while ensuring you develop all the skills needed to pass your test and drive safely for life.
